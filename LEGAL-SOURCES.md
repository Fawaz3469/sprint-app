# Where the legal drafts come from

DRAFT, not legal advice. The Terms and Privacy pages are original plain-English text, structured and adapted from two open sources. No company's proprietary policy text was used, and Automattic's documents (CC BY-SA) were not used.

| Page / section | Source |
|---|---|

| Terms: "Rules", "Other websites and autofill", "Backups and accuracy", "No AI features" | Original, written for what SPRINT does |
| Terms: "Changes, suspension and ending" | Basecamp `terms` (Cancellation and Termination) and GitHub site-policy section M (CC0) |

| Terms: "As is", "Limit of liability", "You protect the Publisher", "Transfer", "Law and courts", "The small print" | GitHub site-policy, liability, indemnification, severability and general clauses (CC0), and Basecamp `terms` (Liability); numbers (₹1,000 cap), Chennai jurisdiction and the successor clause are original |
| Privacy: structure ("What we collect and why", "When we disclose", "Your rights", "Data retention", "Changes and questions") | Basecamp `privacy` (CC BY 4.0) |
