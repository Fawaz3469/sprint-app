Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026 (version 2026-10-27-2)

In short: SPRINT stores your email, your name and your team's hackathon data so it can sign you in, sync with your teammates and work offline. We do not sell your data, show ads, run analytics or use cookies.

"SPRINT" means the SPRINT desktop app and this website. "The Publisher" means Mohammed Fawaz, the individual operating SPRINT, and any successor or entity to which the Publisher transfers SPRINT. This policy goes with the Terms.

What we collect, and why

Your account

Your email address and the display name you choose. We use them to sign you in and to show your name to teammates.

What you and your team add

Teams, team members and roles, hackathons (names, dates, links, organizer), tasks, ideas and votes, submission checklists, notes, your status and progress, templates, and a history of progress. We store this so your team can share it and so SPRINT can sync. Everyone in a team can see that team's data.

Sign-in emails

When you ask for a code, we send an email with a one-time code. Email delivery goes through Google (Gmail SMTP), so Google handles your address and that message.

On your device

  • A copy of your teams' data in a local database (SQLite), so SPRINT works offline.
  • Your sign-in token, kept in the operating system's keychain (macOS Keychain, Windows Credential Manager).
  • Preferences (theme, accent), and the version of the Terms and Privacy Policy you accepted and when.
  • Diagnostic logs on your device. They are not sent to us unless you copy them and send them yourself.

Autofill from a link

When you paste a hackathon link (for example Devpost or Unstop), the app requests that public page from your own device. That website can see your IP address and the request, as with any visit. We do not receive the page. We only store what you save.

Update checks

The app contacts GitHub (this website and the SPRINT releases) when it starts and every few hours to check whether a new version exists, and downloads it if you choose to install. These requests send no personal data and no account details. GitHub can see your IP address and the request, as with any visit.

This website

The website is static. It sets no cookies and has no analytics, ads or trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages and installers are hosted on GitHub Releases, so GitHub may keep standard server logs under its own policy.

Who handles your data

  • Supabase hosts the database, sign-in and live sync.
  • Google delivers sign-in emails (Gmail SMTP).
  • GitHub hosts this website and the installers.

These providers may process data on servers outside India. We do not sell your data or share it for advertising. We may disclose it if the law requires.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. Items you delete in SPRINT are marked as deleted and are not erased automatically, and backups held by our providers may last a little longer. They are removed when we handle a deletion request.

Deleting your data

There is no delete button in the app yet. To have your account and personal data deleted, email help.sprint7@gmail.com from the address you signed in with. We will reply, and aim to complete deletion within 30 days. Content that teammates rely on, such as a task you created in a shared team, may be kept without your name. Uninstalling the app removes only the local copy: see "Uninstall" in Install help.

Security

Connections to the server use HTTPS, your sign-in token lives in the system keychain, and the database limits each team's data to that team's members. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Children

SPRINT is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you are under 18, use SPRINT only with a parent or guardian's permission. If you think a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

Changes and questions

If we change this policy, we will say so in the app and ask you to agree again. Questions or requests: help.sprint7@gmail.com.
