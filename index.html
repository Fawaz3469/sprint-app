Install help

SPRINT is not code-signed yet, so your computer shows a warning the first time. It is safe to continue. You sign in inside the app: accounts are created there with an email code.

macOS

  1. Open the .dmg and drag SPRINT into Applications.
  2. Right-click (or Control-click) SPRINT and choose Open, then Open again.
  3. If you only see "Move to Bin": open System Settings, Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

Windows

  1. Run the installer.
  2. On "Windows protected your PC", click More info.
  3. Click Run anyway.

Uninstall

Removing the app and removing your data are separate steps.

macOS

  1. Quit SPRINT, then drag it from Applications to the Bin.
  2. To remove local data, delete the folders named app.sprint.desktop in ~/Library/Application Support, ~/Library/Logs, ~/Library/Caches and ~/Library/WebKit.
  3. To remove your sign-in, open Keychain Access, search for app.sprint.desktop and delete the entries.

Windows

  1. Open Settings, Apps, Installed apps, find SPRINT and choose Uninstall.
  2. To remove local data, delete the app.sprint.desktop folders in %APPDATA% and %LOCALAPPDATA%.
  3. To remove your sign-in, open Credential Manager, Windows Credentials, and remove entries named app.sprint.desktop.

Uninstalling does not delete your account or team data stored in the cloud. To have it deleted, email help.sprint7@gmail.com from the address you signed in with. See the Privacy Policy.
