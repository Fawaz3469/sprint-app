Open the SPRINT app to sign in. Accounts are created inside the app with an email code.

Built for the team behind the submission

  • 1

    Paste a link, skip the typing

    Drop in a Devpost or Unstop page and SPRINT fills in the name, dates and link for you.

  • 2

    One board for everything

    Tasks on a drag-and-drop board, ideas the team votes on, and a submission checklist so nothing is forgotten.

  • 3

    See who is where

    A progress ring, an honest health label (on track, at risk, blocked) and a quick status from each teammate.

  • 4

    Works offline, stays in sync

    Edit on a train or a bad Wi-Fi. Changes sync with your teammates when you reconnect. Join a team with a short invite code.

Install help

SPRINT is not code-signed yet, so your computer shows a warning the first time. It is safe to continue.

macOS

  1. Open the .dmg and drag SPRINT into Applications.
  2. Right-click (or Control-click) SPRINT and choose Open, then Open again.
  3. If you only see "Move to Bin": open System Settings, Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

Windows

  1. Run the installer.
  2. On "Windows protected your PC", click More info.
  3. Click Run anyway.
